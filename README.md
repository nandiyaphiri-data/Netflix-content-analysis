# 📊 Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This is an independent data analytics portfolio project analysing a publicly available Netflix titles dataset.

The project examines Netflix's content catalogue to identify patterns and trends across content type, genres, countries, ratings and release years.

The analysis was completed using **Power BI**, with the final insights presented through three interactive Power BI dashboards.

> **Disclaimer:** This is an independent portfolio project using a publicly available dataset. It is not affiliated with, commissioned by, or based on Netflix's confidential or internal business data.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project was designed to:

- Analyse the composition of Netflix's content library
- Compare Movies and TV Shows
- Identify leading content-producing countries
- Analyse genre and rating distribution
- Examine content growth over time
- Identify geographic concentration within the content catalogue
- Identify recent changes in content growth
- Translate analytical findings into business-oriented insights and recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tool Used

- **Power BI** – Data cleaning, Validation, Preparation, Data modelling, DAX, visualization and dashboard development

# 📊 Dashboard 1: Netflix Content Overview

### Purpose

This dashboard provides an overall view of Netflix's content catalogue, covering content volume, content type, genres, countries, ratings and content growth over time.

### Key Metrics

- **8,790** total content titles
- **6,126** Movies
- **2,664** TV Shows
- **85** countries represented
- **4,524** directors represented
- Movies account for approximately **69.7%** of the catalogue
- TV Shows account for approximately **30.3%**

### Key Insights

**1. Movies dominate the Netflix catalogue**

Movies represent approximately 69.7% of the analysed catalogue, compared with 30.3% for TV Shows. This indicates a substantially larger movie portfolio within the dataset.

**2. The United States leads content volume**

The United States has the largest content contribution with **3,239 titles**, followed by India with **1,057** and the United Kingdom with **638**.

**3. Content is concentrated among a small number of countries**

The United States, India and the United Kingdom collectively account for approximately **56.1% of the total catalogue** represented in the dataset.

**4. Drama and documentary-related categories are prominent**

Drama, documentary and stand-up-related categories appear among the leading genres in the dataset, highlighting strong representation of these content categories.

**5.TV-MA and TV-14 ratings are prominent**

TV-MA and TV-14 are the most common content ratings, showing a strong presence of mature-oriented content.

**6. Content additions increased significantly from 2010, highlighting Netflix’s expansion of its content library.

### Strategic Insight

The analysis suggests opportunities to further diversify content across countries and genres, while using audience ratings and content trends to guide acquisition and regional content strategies.

# 🌍 Dashboard 2: Global Content Distribution

### Purpose

This dashboard analyses the geographic distribution of Netflix content and highlights differences in content volume across countries and regions.

### Key Insights

**1. The United States is the largest content contributor**

The United States contributes **3,239 titles**, substantially more than the second-largest contributor, India with **1,057 titles**.

**2. The top three countries account for approximately 56.1% of the catalogue**

The United States, India and the United Kingdom collectively represent more than half of the content captured in the dataset.

**3. Netflix's content distribution is geographically uneven**

The map demonstrates that content is concentrated in several major markets, while many countries have considerably smaller representation.

**4. Significant differences exist between major content markets**

The gap between the United States and other major contributors indicates a highly concentrated content portfolio rather than an evenly distributed global catalogue.

### Strategic Considerations

The geographic distribution suggests potential opportunities for analysing underrepresented markets and evaluating how local content could contribute to broader geographic diversification.

# 📈 Dashboard 3: Content Growth & Trend Analysis

### Purpose

This dashboard examines how Netflix's content catalogue has changed over time, with particular focus on annual content volume, year-on-year growth and the relationship between Movies and TV Shows.

### Key Metrics

- **Latest release year represented:** 2021
- **Latest year content:** 592 titles
- **Latest YoY growth:** -37.88%
- **Average annual growth:** 21.32%

### Key Insights

**1. Netflix experienced significant content expansion**

The dashboard shows substantial growth in content volume during the 2010s, with the catalogue expanding rapidly compared with earlier years.

**2. Growth peaked during the expansion period**

The highest year-on-year growth shown in the dashboard occurs during the mid-2010s, reaching approximately **63.38% in 2016**.

**3. Recent content growth has slowed**

The latest year represented in the dataset contains **592 titles**, with year-on-year growth of approximately **-37.88%**, indicating a significant decline in new additions compared with the previous year.

**4. Movies consistently outnumber TV Shows**

Across the period analysed, Movies generally remain above TV Shows in annual content volume.

**5. Content growth is geographically concentrated**

The United States remains the largest contributor, followed by India, the United Kingdom and Pakistan among the leading countries shown in the dashboard.

### Strategic Considerations

The observed slowdown in recent content growth provides an opportunity to investigate changes in content strategy, production cycles and catalogue composition. Further analysis could examine whether the decline differs by genre, country or content type.

# 💡 Overall Business Insights

The three dashboards collectively highlight several important patterns:

### 1. Strong historical content expansion

Netflix's catalogue experienced substantial expansion, particularly during the 2010s.

### 2. Movie-heavy catalogue

Movies represent approximately **69.7%** of the analysed content, making them the dominant content type.

### 3. Geographic concentration

The United States, India and the United Kingdom account for approximately **56.1%** of the total catalogue, indicating significant concentration among major content markets.

### 4. Recent growth slowdown

The latest year represented in the dataset shows a **37.88% year-on-year decline** in content volume.

### 5. Opportunity for geographic diversification

The uneven global distribution of content highlights potential areas for further investigation into underrepresented markets and local content strategies.
